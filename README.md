## 📖 What Is tenten?

Most fonts treat Chinese, Japanese, and Korean as separate worlds. Each one has its own design rules, its own stroke weights, and its own sense of proportion. Put them side by side in one document and the page looks messy — characters jump in size, weight, and style.

tenten fixes that. It is a single font family where every character — whether it is a Traditional Chinese character with twenty strokes or a Japanese kana with two — is drawn on the exact same 12 × 12 dot grid. That shared grid gives you perfect visual consistency across languages, plus a charming retro computer look that feels like an old handheld game screen or an early LED sign.

Because it is a *variable* font, tenten is not a stack of separate files. It is one flexible font that changes weight and style on demand, so you get many looks from one download.

## 🈶 What tenten Covers

tenten was designed from the beginning to serve East Asian writing, not to bolt it on as an afterthought.

- **Traditional Chinese** — the character forms used in Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Macau.
- **Simplified Chinese** — the character forms used in mainland China, Singapore, and Malaysia.
- **Japanese** — kanji together with hiragana and katakana, so full sentences work.
- **Korean** — Hangul syllables rendered in the same dot style.
- **Latin letters, numbers, and punctuation** — so you can mix English, prices, dates, and symbols into the same design.

That means a menu with Chinese dish names, a Japanese subtitle, and a Korean label can all sit on one screen and look like a single, deliberate design choice instead of a patchwork.

## 🎨 Why a 12 × 12 Lattice?

Twelve dots across and twelve dots down is a sweet spot. It is large enough to fit complex characters with many strokes while still keeping the blocky, dot-based charm that makes pixel fonts appealing. It is also small enough that the font stays light and fast.

Because every character uses the same grid, the *rhythm* of your text stays even. Lines of text align cleanly, columns of numbers stay in order, and mixed-script paragraphs no longer wobble up and down. Designers call this consistent optical size, but you will notice it simply as text that looks neat.

## ❓ Common Questions

**Do I need to know programming to use tenten?**
No. The font works like any other font on your computer. Install it once and it appears in the font menu of your writing and design programs.

**Can I use tenten in a commercial project?**
Yes. tenten is released as an open project, so it can be used in personal and commercial work alike.

**Will it work in my web browser?**
Yes. The WOFF2 format is designed specifically for websites and loads quickly.

**What if the characters look blurry?**
Pixel-style fonts look best when they are not stretched or squeezed. Use whole-number sizes when you can, and avoid distorting the text box.

**Can I change the weight?**
Yes. Because tenten is a variable font, supported programs let you slide between lighter and heavier versions without installing anything extra.
